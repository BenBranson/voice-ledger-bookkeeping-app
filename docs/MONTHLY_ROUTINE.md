# Monthly Routine — what to do and say for each client
Benjamin Branson Bookkeeping · first draft 2026-10-01 · mark it up after a few real months, then we'll reorder from your actual voice history

**The rule of the order:** get the data in → categorize → reconcile → review exceptions → report. Findings come *after* the bank work, because many findings disappear once the bank activity is entered.
**Every number she says comes from the client's books. [YOU] = a step you do yourself (she can't).**
**Hands-free version: say "start month-end" and she walks you through section A step by step (see Voice Commands, section 8).**

---

## A. Normal recurring month (about 30 minutes)

| # | Say | What it does | Why now |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"when did we last sync"** | Says how fresh the data is. | Never review stale numbers. |
| 2 | **"go to bank feed cleanup"** | Opens statement lines with nothing posted in QuickBooks. | Get the data in first. |
| 3 | **[YOU]** | In QuickBooks: add or match every missing bank line, then **press Sync**. | Voice never enters transactions. |
| 4 | **"show me the uncategorized transactions"** | Count and total of items with no real category. | Categorize before reconciling. |
| 5 | **[YOU]** | In QuickBooks: reconcile each bank and credit-card account to its statement. | QuickBooks has no way for the app to confirm this. |
| 6 | **"status update"** | Open findings and dollars. | Now see what's left. |
| 7 | **"pull up the duplicates"** | Duplicate pairs and dollars at risk. | |
| 8 | **"pull up the negative balances"** then **"suspense"** | Overdrawn accounts; suspense and clearing balances. | These should be zero at month end. |
| 9 | **"who owes us"** | Receivables: gross, credits, net, how much is 60+ days old. | Collections. |
| 10 | **"what do we owe"** | Payables total and past-due. | Bills. |
| 11 | **"what's our cash balance"** | Total bank balance. | Compare to the statements. |
| 12 | **"what's our revenue this month"** then **"what's our net income"** | The month's result. | Sanity check before sending. |
| 13 | **"go to month end close"** | The month-end checklist. | Attest each step. |
| 14 | **"go to close package"** | The client report. | Last. Send only if the confidence rating isn't Low. |

## B. New client — catch-up (before any regular month)

| # | Say | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"go to client diagnostics"** | The 24-month picture: trends, problem months. |
| 2 | **"go to cleanup assessment"** | Total dollar exposure and recommended cleanup scope — the basis for your quote. |
| 3 | **"pull up the negative balances"** then **"suspense"** | The biggest signs of a messy file. |
| 4 | Work the **oldest** month first: steps 2–5 of the table above, then **"go to month end close"**. | Finish and reconcile one month completely before starting the next — balances carry forward. |
| 5 | Repeat month by month until you reach the current month, then switch to routine A. | |
| 6 | **Do not send a client report** for any month that isn't reconciled. | The report's confidence rating will say Low until it is. |

*Not in the list because the app can't do them by voice: entering transactions, reconciling, changing QuickBooks. Those always happen in QuickBooks, by you.*
