# Refund customers with a refund receipt

## Purpose / when to use
Record money actually paid back to a customer. (A credit memo is store credit, not a refund.)

## Steps in QBO
Payment taken through QuickBooks Payments: open the original sale. For an invoice, click the payment link; for a sales receipt, open it. Then More > Refund > "Yes, refund payment". QBO fills the refund receipt from the sale; check it and save.

Payment taken any other way: + New > Refund receipt > customer (optional) > date > payment method used to return the money > Refund from account (usually the bank) > check number if by check > the product/service and amount (sales tax is added if it applies) > save.

## Pitfalls
- If the payment went through an outside processor (Stripe, PayPal), you must still issue the refund there; the refund receipt only records it.
- When the refund comes through the bank or card feed, match it to the refund receipt so it isn't recorded twice.

## How to verify
In the bank feed, the downloaded refund is matched/confirmed against the refund receipt.
