# Create invoices

## Purpose / when to use
Bill a customer who will pay later. (If they pay on the spot, use a sales receipt.)

## Steps in QBO
1. + Create > Invoice, or Sales > Invoices > New invoice.
2. Choose the customer (or + Add New).
3. Check Invoice date, Due date and Terms ("Net 30" = due 30 days after the invoice date).
4. Add product/service lines: flat rate, hourly or per item; quantity and rate.
5. Manage (gear) panel: Customization (which fields show), Scheduling (make recurring, automatic reminders), Payment options, Design.
6. Review and send to email it, or Save to send later.

## Prerequisites and plan limits
Limited on Free and Lite. Online payment options need a connected bank account. Sales tax applies automatically when set up and the item is taxable.

## How to verify
Status shows Sent and the customer receives the email.
