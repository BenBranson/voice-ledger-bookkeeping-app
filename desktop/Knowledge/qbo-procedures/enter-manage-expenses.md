# Enter and manage expenses in QuickBooks Online

## Purpose / when to use
Record costs that have already been paid. If you will pay later, enter a bill instead.

## Steps in QBO
Record an expense:
1. + Create > Expense.
2. Payee: the vendor (can be left blank when recording several petty cash items).
3. Payment account, Payment date, Payment method.
4. Optional: Ref no. or Permit no.; Tags.
5. Category details: pick the expense account in Category and add a description. Use Item details only for inventory purchases.
6. Amount and Tax.
7. To re-bill a customer: tick Billable and enter the Customer.
8. Optional Memo > Save and close.

Edit: All apps > Expenses & Bills > Expense transactions > View/Edit > change > Save and close.

Delete: All apps > Expenses & Bills > Expense transactions > View/Edit dropdown > Delete > confirm (twice).

Review re-billed expenses: Reports > Standard reports > Income by Customer Summary.

## Prerequisites and plan limits
Listed for Simple Start, Essentials, Plus, Advanced, Solopreneur Plus, Free and Lite.

## Pitfalls
- Editing a billable expense affects invoices sent afterwards.
- A deleted expense disappears from reports but remains in the Audit log.
- Use Item details only when buying inventory.

## How to verify
The expense appears in Expense transactions; billable ones show in Income by Customer Summary.
