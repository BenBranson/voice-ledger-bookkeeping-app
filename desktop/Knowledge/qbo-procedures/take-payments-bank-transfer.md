# Accept bank transfer payments on invoices (QuickBooks Payments)

## Purpose / when to use
Let customers pay an invoice by bank transfer from the invoice email.

## Steps in QBO
- Create > Invoice; payment options normally show automatically. If not, open the Manage panel > Payment options > turn on the bank transfer option. Complete and Review and send; the customer gets a pay link.
- Tracking: search for Payouts to see daily deposit summaries, each sale and its fee (fees appear as separate transactions). Deposits usually arrive in 3 to 5 business days. Then Accounting > Bank transactions > Match the deposit to the bank feed.

## Prerequisites and limits
The business must first be approved for QuickBooks Payments card processing. The article gives a 1% per-transaction fee for bank transfers.

## How to verify
The payout deposit is matched in Bank transactions.
