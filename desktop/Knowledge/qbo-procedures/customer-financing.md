# Customer financing option on estimates

## Purpose / when to use
Lets a client's customers apply for third-party financing (through Intuit Credit Karma's marketplace) on larger estimates, to help estimates get accepted. Offered on estimates over $1,000.

## Steps in QBO
Settings > Account and settings > Sales > Customer financing section > Edit > toggle Customer financing on (or off) > Save > Done.

## Prerequisites and limits
US individual consumers only (not business borrowers); on by default for eligible businesses; minimum amounts apply; no cost to the business.

## Pitfalls
Business email addresses may stop the offer from showing. Checking rates is a soft credit inquiry; accepting a loan triggers a hard inquiry on the customer's credit.

## How to verify
Check the toggle under Account and settings > Sales.
