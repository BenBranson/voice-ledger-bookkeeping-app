# Enter bills in QuickBooks Online

## Purpose / when to use
Record vendor invoices you have not paid yet, so accounts payable is tracked. If the expense is already paid, record it as an expense instead of a bill.

## Steps in QBO
Enter a bill by hand:
1. + Create > Bill.
2. Choose the Vendor.
3. Choose Terms.
4. Enter Bill date, Due date and Bill no. exactly as on the vendor's bill.
5. Fill in Category details.
6. Optional: fill Item details to itemize products/services.
7. Enter the Amount and any tax.
8. Optional: tick Billable and enter the Customer to pass the cost on.
9. Save and close.

Upload bills:
1. All apps > Expenses & Bills > Bills.
2. Add bill dropdown > Upload from computer.
3. Drag and drop or browse. Accepted: PDF, JPEG, JPG, GIF, PNG.
4. Review and confirm the extracted details before scheduling payment.

Bills from the QuickBooks Business Network:
Vendors who use QuickBooks can send bills directly; QuickBooks reads the vendor details and notifies you in the Bills list for review.

## Prerequisites and plan limits
- Need the vendor details and the bill document.
- To itemize: Gear > Account and settings > Expenses > Bills and expenses > Show Items table on expense and purchase forms.
- To bill customers for time: Gear > Account and settings > Time > Timesheet > Billable time.
- Plans listed: Simple Start, Essentials, Plus, Advanced.

## Pitfalls
- Already-paid costs are expenses, not bills (entering both double-counts).
- Uploaded and network bills land in the For review tab; hand-entered bills go straight to Unpaid.

## How to verify
Bills page: hand-entered bills are in Unpaid; uploaded/network bills wait in For review until accepted.
