# Filter, search, and sort with custom fields

Linked from the custom-fields hub page.

## Purpose / when to use
Use custom fields to sort invoice, customer and expense lists, narrow searches, and filter reports (e.g. by sales rep or loyalty program).

## Steps in QBO
Add a custom field column to a list and sort:
1. Open the list:
   - Invoices: All apps > Sales & Get Paid > Invoices
   - Customers: All apps > Customer Hub > Customers & leads
   - Expenses: All apps > Expenses & Bills > Expense transactions
2. Gear on the list itself > tick the custom field > close the gear.
3. Click the column header to sort alphabetically.

Search by custom field:
1. Search (magnifying glass) > type the custom field name.
2. Pick a result, or choose Advanced Search.
3. In Advanced Search, pick the custom field from the dropdown next to Contains or Equals > Search.

## Prerequisites and plan limits
QBO Advanced. The custom fields must already exist (Gear > Custom fields).

## Pitfalls
Not available below Advanced.

## How to verify
The field appears as a sortable column, and Advanced Search returns the expected transactions.
