# Understand how the QuickBooks Business Network works

## Purpose / when to use
The Business Network lets QuickBooks users find and connect with their customers and vendors and exchange invoices and bills directly, so documents arrive in each other's books without manual re-entry.

## Steps in QBO
The article gives a four-stage overview, not menu paths (see business-network-connect-contacts.md for clicks):
1. Verify your information: keep company contact details current; connections see them after accepting.
2. Connect: find the vendor or customer in the network and send an invitation.
3. Exchange: once accepted, invoices you send land in the customer's account and vendors' bills arrive in yours automatically.
4. Stay updated: contact details sync automatically when a connection changes theirs.

## Prerequisites and plan limits
- Company information must be current, and the customers/vendors must already exist in the file.
- Listed for Simple Start, Essentials, Plus, Advanced, Accountant, Enterprise Suite, Lite and Free.
- Accountant Suite users can manage clients' network visibility.

## Pitfalls
Applying to the Business Network triggers a bundled eligibility review for QuickBooks Payments and QuickBooks Payroll.
