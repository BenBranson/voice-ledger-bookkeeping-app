# Accounts payable automation (review bills received and uploaded)

Note: this URL is titled "import invoices and bills" but on 2026-09-27 it served an article on accounts payable automation. No spreadsheet import procedure was on the page.

## Purpose / when to use
When a vendor sends an invoice through the QuickBooks Business Network (or you upload a bill), QuickBooks reads the vendor details and places a draft bill on the Bills page for you to review, approve and add to the books, cutting manual entry.

## Steps in QBO
Review a pending bill:
1. All apps > Expenses & Bills > Bills > For review tab.
2. Review bill on the pending item.
3. Check the extracted details; choose the Account/Category; adjust anything else.
4. Save and schedule payment, Save and close, or Delete this bill.

Upload a bill:
1. All apps > Expenses & Bills > Bills > Add bill dropdown > Upload from computer.
2. Drag and drop or Upload to browse (PDF, JPEG, JPG, GIF, PNG).
3. The bill appears in For review.

## Prerequisites and plan limits
- Simple Start, Essentials, Plus and Advanced; still rolling out, so may not be in every account yet.
- Receiving invoices electronically requires the QuickBooks Business Network.

## Pitfalls
- Feature may be missing from some accounts during rollout.

## How to verify
A saved bill carries a PDF of the vendor's original invoice as an attachment; it then appears with other bills for payment.
