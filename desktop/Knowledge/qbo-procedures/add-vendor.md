# Add a vendor or contractor in QuickBooks

## Purpose / when to use
Set up the people and companies the business owes money to, including subcontractors, so bills and payments can be tracked against them.

## Steps in QBO
Add one vendor:
1. All apps > Expenses & Bills > Vendors.
2. New vendor.
3. Fill in the Vendor information window: Name (required), Email (you can add CC/BCC addresses used on transactions), Notes (multiple notes allowed; you can comment on a note and mark favourites), plus any other useful details.
4. If the vendor will need a 1099, tick the 1099 tracking box during setup.
5. Save.

Import many vendors:
1. All apps > Expenses & Bills > Vendors.
2. Import Vendors.
3. Attach the Excel spreadsheet, map its columns to QuickBooks fields, review, then Import.

Make a vendor inactive:
1. All apps > Expenses & Bills > Vendors.
2. In the dropdown beside the vendor, choose Make inactive.

## Prerequisites and plan limits
Available across QBO plans.

## Pitfalls
- Vendors cannot be deleted, only made inactive.
- For duplicates, merge the vendors instead (see merge-duplicate-vendors.md).

## How to verify
The vendor appears in the Vendors list; opening it shows balance due and transaction history.
