# Add purchase orders to expenses, bills, or checks in QuickBooks Online

## Purpose / when to use
When the vendor ships against an accepted PO, record the bill, expense or check and link the PO to it. Linking closes the PO (fully or partly) and keeps the accounting tied together.

## Steps in QBO
Settings first:
1. Gear > Account and settings > Expenses.
2. Bills and expenses > Edit (pencil).
3. Turn on: Show Items table on expense and purchase forms; Track expenses and items by customer; Make expenses and items billable.
4. Save > Done.

Add a full PO:
1. + Create > Expense, Check or Bill.
2. Pick the vendor in Payee. A panel lists that vendor's open POs.
3. Add on the correct PO. Its lines fill the Item details section.
4. For billable lines, tick Billable and choose the Customer.
5. Save and close.

Add part of a PO:
Same as above, but after Add, change the quantity or amount on each line to what was actually received/billed, then Save and close.

Unlink a PO from a saved transaction:
- Before saving: on the PO line, open the Action column dropdown and choose Delete. Only that line is removed.
- After saving: All apps > Expenses & Bills > Expense transactions > open the bill/expense > on the linked PO line choose Delete from its dropdown. This removes the linked line, not the whole transaction.
- Then reopen the PO: open the purchase order, change the status (shown as "Closed" near the vendor) to Open, and Save.

## Prerequisites and plan limits
- Purchase orders are QBO Plus and Advanced only.
- Itemization must be on for bills (Show Items table setting).

## Pitfalls
- QuickBooks closes a PO automatically once all its quantities have been added to transactions.
- Several transactions can draw from one PO (partial receipts).
- Removing the link does not reopen the PO; you must reopen it yourself.

## How to verify
The PO status changes to Closed when fully used; open PO reports (see create-send-purchase-orders.md) show what remains.
