# Learn about QuickBooks Bill Pay

## Purpose / when to use
Overview of QuickBooks Bill Pay, the built-in service that records vendor bills and sends payments from QBO. Explains tiers, delivery methods and fee timing. No click-by-click steps (see bill-pay-activate-subscribe.md to sign up).

## Steps in QBO
Not provided in this article.

## Prerequisites and plan limits
- A basic level of Bill Pay comes with standard QuickBooks plans.
- Premium and Elite tiers add unlimited 1099 e-filing at no extra cost.
- Custom user roles and multi-step bill approval workflows come with Elite, or with QBO Advanced.

## Delivery times and fees
- Standard ACH: 3-5 business days.
- Faster ACH: 1 business day, for eligible customers, extra fee, maximum $25,000 per payment. Must be scheduled by 5 PM PT on a business day; weekends and federal holidays do not count.
- Paper check: 8-10 business days.
- Subscription fee is charged at the start of the billing cycle; processing fees are charged at the end of the month for that month's payments.

## Pitfalls
- Missing the 5 PM PT cutoff means no next-day delivery.
- Delivery can slip because of third-party delays or risk reviews.
