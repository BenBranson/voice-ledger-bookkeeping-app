# Convert an estimate to an invoice

## Purpose / when to use
Copies an accepted estimate's details into a new invoice and links the two.

## Steps in QBO
All apps > Sales & Get Paid > Sales transactions > find the estimate > Action column: Convert to invoice > review and edit > Save.

## Prerequisites
- The estimate's status must be Accepted.
- If the estimate asked for a deposit, the customer must pay it (and accept) before conversion is offered.
- A Rejected estimate can't be converted: set it back to Pending and resend it for acceptance.

## How to verify
The new invoice appears in Sales transactions carrying the estimate's lines and is linked to the estimate.
