# Activate and subscribe to QuickBooks Bill Pay

## Purpose / when to use
Enrol a business in QuickBooks Bill Pay so vendors can be paid and tracked from QBO. The application takes under about 10 minutes and should be done by the business owner. Applying triggers a bundled eligibility review covering Bill Pay, Payments, Payroll and the Business Network.

## Steps in QBO
1. Expenses > Bills > Schedule Payment.
2. Pick a tier: Basic (included with the subscription), Premium or Elite.
3. Complete the application:
   - Business information: website, industry, business type, phone.
   - Owner information: first/last name, mobile number, last four digits of SSN, date of birth, address.
   - Payment method: find the business bank account bills will be paid from and connect it with the bank sign-in.
4. Review > Finish setup.
5. Confirm how the subscription will be paid > Subscribe.

## Prerequisites and plan limits
Have ready:
- Business phone, address and EIN (must match IRS records).
- Owner's date of birth, home address and last four SSN digits (the full SSN may be requested).
- The bank account for paying bills.
- A bank account or credit card for the subscription charge.
The article says Basic includes five free ACH payments; higher tiers add more.

## Pitfalls
- Most applications are approved immediately. If more verification is needed, a decision email comes within about 3 to 5 days.
- The article says the business owner should complete the application (it asks for the owner's identity details).

## How to verify
After subscribing, schedule-payment options are available from the Bills section.
