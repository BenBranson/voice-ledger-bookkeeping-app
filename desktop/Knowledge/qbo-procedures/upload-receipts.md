# Upload your receipts to QuickBooks

## Purpose / when to use
Get receipt and bill images into QBO. QuickBooks reads the data, creates a transaction for review, and can match it to an existing (e.g. bank feed) transaction, keeping records audit-ready.

## Steps in QBO
From a browser:
All apps > Accounting > Receipts > Upload from computer (or Upload from Google Drive) > choose the file.

From the mobile app (iOS/Android):
Menu (≡) > Receipt snap > Receipt camera > take the photo > Use this photo > Done.

By email forwarding (admin sets up):
1. Receipts > Forward from email.
2. Choose a custom forwarding address name > Next > confirm the address > Done.
3. In Manage forwarding email, switch on which users may forward.
4. Users send receipts from the email address they sign in to QuickBooks with.

Review and match:
1. Receipts > For review tab > open the receipt.
2. If QuickBooks found matches, See suggested matches > pick the transaction > Match. If there is a match you can go straight to Match without reviewing.
3. Otherwise complete the transaction details, or use Search manually to find one.
4. Optional Memo > Save and close. Finished receipts move to the Reviewed tab.

## Prerequisites and plan limits
- File types: PDF, JPEG, JPG, GIF, PNG. iPhone/iPad HEIC photos may need converting.
- Black out credit card numbers and government ID numbers before uploading.
- Free and Lite plans have limited receipt features (upgrade via Gear > Subscriptions and billing > Upgrade your plan).
- Employee expense claims need QBO Advanced.
- Mobile capture needs the QuickBooks app from the App Store / Google Play.

## Pitfalls
- One receipt per file. Several receipts in one email are fine only as separate image attachments.
- Dark or blurry images extract slowly or fail.
- Only an administrator can set up email forwarding, and emails must come from addresses registered in QuickBooks.

## How to verify
The For review tab shows how many matches were found; after saving, the receipt sits in Reviewed and the transaction has the receipt attached.
