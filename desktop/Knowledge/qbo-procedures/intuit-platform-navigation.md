# Explore QuickBooks on the Intuit Platform

## Purpose / when to use
Orientation for the redesigned QBO interface on the Intuit platform. Use it to find where familiar screens moved. It has no task procedures; it describes the new layout.

## Steps in QBO (where things are now)
- Apps: features such as Expenses and Inventory are opened as "Intuit apps" from an app carousel / sidebar rather than nested menus.
- Create actions: a customizable area for the transactions you create most often.
- Sales transactions live under "Sales & Get Paid"; expense transactions under "Expenses & Bills".
- Bookmarks: hover the bookmarks icon in the sidebar to reach saved favourite pages.
- Global search on the homepage finds transactions, contacts, reports and help content.
- Accounting section: "Bank transactions" is listed first, and the section also holds bank accounts, registers, reconciliation and the chart of accounts.
- Reports: hover the reports icon in the sidebar for standard reports, custom reports and financial planning.

## Prerequisites and plan limits
No plan restrictions stated; applies across QBO subscription tiers.

## Pitfalls
The article's message is that screens were relocated, not removed. If a familiar feature seems missing, look for it under the app carousel or search.

## How to verify
Not covered by the article.

## Bookkeeper note (not from source)
Many older help articles and videos still use old paths (e.g. "Expenses > Vendors"). In the new layout the same screen is usually "All apps > Expenses & Bills > Vendors".
