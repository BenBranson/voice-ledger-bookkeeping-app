# Set up sales settings

## Purpose / when to use
Controls what sales forms capture, default terms, automation and customer messages. Several tracking options are off by default, so review each one when setting up a client.

## Steps in QBO
Settings > Account and settings > Sales tab. Edit each section with its pencil icon:
- Sales form content: turn fields on/off (e.g. Shipping), set Preferred invoice terms, and Preferred delivery method (print later or email).
- Products and services: Show SKU column; inventory tracking (Plus and Advanced only); Price rules (automatic pricing by date or customer type); Progress invoicing (bill an estimate in several installments).
- Messages: default email templates, copy yourself on sent emails, unpaid-balance reminder text.
- Delivery / statements: how much detail emailed invoices and receipts show, whether a PDF is attached; for statements, whether to list items and show aging.
Save.

## Prerequisites and plan limits
Inventory tracking needs QBO Plus or Advanced. Service-only businesses can ignore inventory options.

## How to verify
Create a test sale and confirm the form shows the fields and defaults you set.
