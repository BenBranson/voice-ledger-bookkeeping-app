# Add, customize or remove logos on sales forms

## Purpose / when to use
Branding for invoices, estimates and sales receipts. Up to 10 logos can be stored for different forms.

## Steps in QBO
- Add: Settings > Custom form styles > New style (or Edit an existing style) > Design tab > Add your unique logo > the + box > choose the image > Done.
- Resize/position: Custom form styles > Edit > Design > Make logo edits > placement Left/Center/Right, size Small/Medium/Large > Done.
- Remove: same path > Make logo edits > Hide > Done.

## Prerequisites and plan limits
Limited on QBO Free and Lite. Location-specific logos need Plus. Image rules: .gif, .bmp, .jpg/.jpe/.jpeg; under 10 MB; 24-bit colour or less; square works best; RGB colour (convert CMYK first).

## Pitfalls
Rectangular or CMYK images can display badly; keep the original image file and save edited copies separately.

## How to verify
Use Print or Preview at the bottom of a sales form.
