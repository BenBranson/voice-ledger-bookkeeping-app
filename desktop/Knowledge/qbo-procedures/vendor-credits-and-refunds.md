# Enter vendor credits and refunds in QuickBooks Online

## Purpose / when to use
Record money or credit a vendor gives back. Which method to use depends on how the original purchase was recorded: expense/check, bill, or credit card charge.

## Steps in QBO
A. Refund of an expense or check (no bill involved):
1. + Create > Bank deposit.
2. Under Add funds to this deposit: Received From = the vendor; Account = the same expense account used on the original purchase; Amount = refund.
3. Optional: tick Track returns for customers and choose the customer/project.
4. Save and close.

B. Vendor credit to use against a future or open bill:
1. + Create > Vendor credit > choose Vendor > enter Category or Item details > Save and close.
2. + Create > Pay bills > select the bill. QuickBooks applies the available credit automatically; check the Credit Applied amount > Save and close.

C. Refund check for a bill already paid:
1. + Create > Vendor credit > Vendor > Category or Item details matching the original bill > Save and close.
2. + Create > Bank deposit > Received From = vendor; Account = Accounts Payable (A/P); Payment Method (e.g. Check); Amount > Save and close.
3. + Create > Pay bills > select the new deposit and the credit. Credit Applied should equal the deposit and Total payment should be $0.00 > Save and close.

D. Refund to a credit card:
1. + Create > Credit card credit.
2. Payee = vendor; choose the credit card account; enter Payment Date, Amount, Tax, and Category (same account as the original expense).
3. Save and close.

## Prerequisites and plan limits
Listed for Simple Start, Essentials, Plus, Advanced, Solopreneur, Free and Lite. No special settings.

## Pitfalls
- In method C the deposit must use Accounts Payable (A/P) as its account, otherwise it will not link to the vendor credit.
- Choosing an inventory item on a vendor credit puts the item back into stock. If the goods were defective or should not return to inventory, make an inventory adjustment.

## How to verify
- A: the deposit shows the vendor and refund amount.
- B/C: in Pay bills, Credit Applied is correct; in C the Total payment is $0.00.
- D: the credit card credit shows the right payee, account and amount.
