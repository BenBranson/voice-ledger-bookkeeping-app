# Create and send purchase orders in QuickBooks Online

## Purpose / when to use
A purchase order (PO) tells a vendor you intend to buy specific products in specific quantities. After the vendor accepts, the PO is later linked to the bill or expense so the records stay connected (see purchase-orders-to-bills-expenses.md).

## Steps in QBO
Turn on purchase orders (first time):
1. Gear > Account and settings > Expenses tab.
2. Purchase orders section > Edit.
3. Switch on "Use purchase orders".
4. Optional: add up to three custom fields and a default message to vendors.
5. Save > Done.

Create and send a PO:
1. + Create > Purchase order.
2. Choose the vendor; check the mailing address.
3. If shipping straight to a customer, pick them in the Ship to dropdown and confirm the shipping address.
4. Enter the purchase order date. Custom fields can be added through the form's gear (Settings).
5. In Item details, add the products. Only items set up with "I purchase this product/service from a vendor" can be selected.
6. Save and send (emails it now) or Save and close (send later).

Send a saved PO later:
All apps > Expenses & Bills > Expense transactions > find the PO > Send in the Action column.

Save a custom PO template:
Form gear > Design dropdown > Other Templates > Add/Edit > New style > Purchase order > make changes > Done.

## Prerequisites and plan limits
- QBO Plus or Advanced only.
- Inventory/purchase items already set up.
- Purchase orders switched on in settings.

## Pitfalls
- Not available on Simple Start or Essentials.
- Items not marked as purchased from a vendor will not appear on the PO.

## How to verify
- A new PO has status Open.
- Reports > Standard reports: Open Purchase Order List, Open Purchase Order Detail, Purchases by Product/Service Detail, Purchases by Vendor Detail.
- Overdue POs: All apps > Inventory > Purchase orders shows Overdue and Open statuses.
