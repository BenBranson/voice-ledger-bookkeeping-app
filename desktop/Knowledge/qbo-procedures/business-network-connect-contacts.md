# Connect with contacts in the QuickBooks Business Network

## Purpose / when to use
Link directly with a customer or vendor in the Business Network so invoices and bills flow automatically between your QuickBooks files.

## Steps in QBO
Check your business contact details (Primary Admin or Company Admin):
1. Gear > Account and settings > Company tab.
2. Confirm or update Company name, Customer-facing email, Company phone, Website, Customer-facing address.
3. Save > Done.

Connect with a contact (on the customer or vendor form):
1. Type the business name in Company name.
2. Pick the matching business from the search results.
3. Save and send request to send the invitation.

## Prerequisites and plan limits
Essentials, Plus and Advanced. Must be signed in as Primary Admin or Admin to update company info.

## Pitfalls
- Until the invitation is accepted, the other business's email, phone and full address are partly hidden.
- Fields showing a link icon are synced from the network; editing them by hand turns off automatic updates.

## How to verify
Once the contact accepts, their full contact details appear in the profile.
