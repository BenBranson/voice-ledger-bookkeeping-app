# Create and apply credit memos and delayed credits

## Purpose / when to use
- Credit memo: reduce what the customer owes now; it works like a payment against open invoices. It affects sales reports as soon as it is created, even before it is applied.
- Delayed credit: record a credit to use on a future invoice. It does not change the open balance or sales reports until it is added to an invoice.

## Settings
Optional auto-apply: Settings > Account and settings > Advanced > Automation. When on, credits apply to the oldest unpaid invoice first, then the next.

## Steps in QBO
Create: + Create > Credit memo or Delayed credit > customer > date, items, amount > Save and close.

Apply a credit memo manually: + Create > Receive payment > customer > tick the invoice under Outstanding transactions > tick the credit under Credits > amount in the Payment column. Leave Payment method, Reference no., Deposit to and Amount received blank > Save.

Apply a delayed credit: + Create > Invoice > choose the customer; the side panel lists available delayed credits > Add > finish > Save and close.

## Pitfalls
- Adding a delayed credit to an invoice dated in a prior period changes that period's balances.
- To undo a wrong auto-application: open the invoice > click the applied credit link > open the payment > More > Delete > confirm.

## How to verify
- Customer Balance Detail shows the invoice and the credit memo as both open if the credit is still unapplied.
- A credit memo with no open invoice to apply to shows Unapplied, and the customer balance goes negative.
