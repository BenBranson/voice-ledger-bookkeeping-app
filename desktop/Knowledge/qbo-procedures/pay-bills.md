# Pay bills in QuickBooks Online

## Purpose / when to use
Record payment of open bills made by check, credit card or cash from inside QBO.

## Steps in QBO
1. Go to the Expenses & Pay Bills area (the article's path starts from the Dashboard) and choose Pay bills.
2. Payment account: choose the checking, savings or credit account the money came from.
3. For a checking/savings account you may enter a Starting check no.
4. Set the Payment date.
5. Tick the bill(s) being paid.
6. Save.

Check what happened to a payment:
1. Gear > Audit log.
2. Filter with the User, Date Changed or Events dropdowns.
3. The History column shows transaction details, including reasons for voids.

## Prerequisites and plan limits
Listed for all QBO plans (Simple Start through Advanced, Solopreneur, Free, Lite, Accountant, Enterprise Suite).

## Pitfalls
None stated in the article.

## How to verify
Use the Audit log as above to confirm the payment and its history.

## Bookkeeper note (not from source)
In the current menu layout Pay bills is also reachable from + Create > Pay bills (used in the vendor-credit article). A bill payment clears the bill from Unpaid; recording a separate expense for the same payment would double-count it.
