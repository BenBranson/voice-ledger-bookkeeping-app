# Set up autopay for recurring invoices

## Purpose / when to use
Customers pay recurring invoices automatically each cycle.

## Prerequisites and limits
QuickBooks Payments; the recurring invoice must be pay-enabled; invoice total $5,000 or less; no daily interval; the customer needs an Intuit account.

## Steps in QBO
1. Recurring template: create the invoice > Make recurring > Interval (weekly, monthly, yearly...) > start/end dates > Save template.
2. The customer opens the invoice email > Set up autopay > signs in > confirms the autopay box, frequency and start date > Autopay (pays the current invoice and enrols future ones).
3. Check status: invoices list > the invoice's Status > Activity panel shows "Autopay Scheduled".

## Pitfalls
- Changing the amount, frequency, terms, payment options, email or customer name cancels autopay.
- Partial payment isn't possible with autopay.
- You can cancel the whole enrolment, not a single payment.
- A failed payment emails the customer, who must update the payment method.

## Timing
Charged 3 days before the due date; immediately if the due date is within 3 days or the invoice is due on receipt.
