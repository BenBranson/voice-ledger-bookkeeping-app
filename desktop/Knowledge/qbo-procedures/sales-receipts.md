# Create and send sales receipts

## Purpose / when to use
Record a sale the customer paid for at the time of sale. If they will pay later, create an invoice instead.

## Steps in QBO
+ Create > Sales receipt > choose customer (optional but recommended for sales-by-customer reporting) > choose product/service, Qty and Rate > payment method (cash, check with number, or add one) > Deposit to (Undeposited Funds if it will be deposited together with other payments; otherwise the bank account) > Save, or Save and send to email it.

Bulk entry: batch import of sales receipts from CSV is an Advanced-plan feature. Before importing, turn on "Show Product/Service column on sales forms" (Settings > Account and settings > Sales > Products and services). The CSV must use MM/DD/YYYY dates; rows with errors are highlighted red.

## How to verify
The saved receipt opens, and if sent, the email subject and body can be edited before sending.
