# A closer look at the new QuickBooks bank feed and Accounting Agent (July 8, 2025)

Author: Michael Hitchcock, Firm of the Future. Published July 8, 2025.

## What it is
A redesigned bank feed (bank transactions page) in QBO and QBOA, plus added Accounting Agent features, built on newer AI instead of the earlier machine-learning approach.

## What it does
Bank feed:
- Inline editing of category, vendor/payee, class, location and product/service, with no pop-up overlay.
- Hover to see the full bank description.
- Categorization suggestions that learn from the full bank description and past transactions with similar vendors.
- Handles one-to-many matches that were not possible before.
- Payee suggestions explain themselves by showing earlier transactions with that vendor; a new payee can be added from the dropdown without leaving the feed.
- Still there: rules, categorization history, splits, exclude, mark as transfer / credit card payment, column customization and resizing, grouping.

Accounting Agent:
- Statement upload: PDF, JPG or PNG bank/card statements; transactions are extracted for confirmation before going into the feed; human review if extraction fails.
- Context gathering (Essentials and up): ask the client about a specific transaction in an AI conversation that stays attached to the transaction; suggestions update with the answer.
- Ready to post: separates transactions with known vendors and history-based category suggestions so easy items can be posted quickly. User-set preferences were planned for later.
- Anomaly detection (Plus and up): flags possible errors or miscategorizations on the Balance Sheet and P&L, with transaction-level explanations.
- Enhanced reconciliation (Plus and up, optional): three-way comparison of the uploaded statement, the books and the bank feed; highlights differences and suggests fixes.

## Availability
- QBO and QBOA. Clients could be opted in or out temporarily (individually or in bulk); by the end of September 2025 the classic experience was retired and opted-out clients moved to the new one.
- Beta tested for almost a year with more than 65,000 accountants and small businesses.

## What it means for a bookkeeper
- Reconciliation: the three-way comparison points to where the statement, feed and books disagree.
- Categorization: suggestions come with reasons, and Ready to post splits routine items from ones needing attention.
- Month-end/cleanup: anomaly detection on the P&L and Balance Sheet surfaces miscategorizations before close.
- Client questions stay on the transaction record.

## Limits / caveats
- Survey figures cited (QBO customers, March/April 2025): 80% of early users said transactions were easier to handle; 45% said they already save 12 hours a month. These are self-reported.
- Anomaly detection and enhanced reconciliation need Plus or higher; context gathering needs Essentials or higher.
- Statement extraction can fail and need human review.
- The accountant decides how much AI help to use.
