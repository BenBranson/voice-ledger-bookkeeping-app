# QuickBooks Bill Pay: tiers and fees (product page, as of 2026-09-27)

Marketing page; prices change. Snapshot taken 2026-09-27.

## Tiers
- Basic: $0/month, included with QuickBooks. Free standard ACH.
- Premium: $15/month (promo $7.50/month for 3 months). Free standard ACH. Adds unlimited 1099 e-filing with printing and mailing.
- Elite: $45/month (promo $22.50/month for 3 months). Free standard ACH. Adds 1099 e-filing, roles and permissions, payment release approval, bill approval automation. Included free with QBO Advanced from August 1, 2026.
- All tiers: AI bill creation, a bills inbox, auto-matching, users per the QuickBooks plan.

## Per-payment fees
- Standard ACH: free, 3-5 business days.
- Check: $1.50 each, 8-10 business days.
- Faster ACH: $10, usually next business day if scheduled by 5 PM PT the business day before.
- Instant payment: 1% of the amount, maximum fee $100, minimum payment $10.

## Conflicts with other sources
- The help article bill-pay-activate-subscribe.md and the Firm of the Future guide say Basic includes five free ACH payments (the guide says per month). This page says standard ACH is free on every tier without stating a cap.
- The help article quickbooks-bill-pay-overview.md gives a $25,000 maximum per Faster ACH payment; this page does not mention a cap.
Check the client's own Bill Pay settings for current terms.
