# ProAdvisor In the Know: Updates to Bank Feeds in QuickBooks Online (August 28, 2025)

Author: Jaclyn Anku, Firm of the Future. Published August 28, 2025.

## What it is
A walkthrough of the redesigned Bank Feed (bank transactions page) in QBO, released after about nine months of beta with up to 70,000 users.

## What it does
Editing and layout:
- Edit transactions directly in the table row; the overlay is still available.
- Hover for the full bank description.
- Choose which columns show; class, location and project can be edited inline.
- A down arrow beside the Action column holds less-used actions (splits, exclusions, rules, history are still there but one click deeper).

Payees:
- Add a vendor/payee without leaving the feed.
- Payee suggestions come with an explanation of how they were chosen.

Categorization suggestions:
- Based on a model personal to the company using 24 months of history (previously 12).
- Each suggestion explains why; if there is no exact vendor history, it looks at similar vendors.
- Several category options may be offered and can be picked from the explanation; past categorization history is still visible.

Matching:
- Reads the bank description to narrow many possible matches to the best one.
- Can handle cases like an international payment with a bank fee by picking up the fee from the description.
- Pulls invoice numbers from the memo to match one payment to several invoices.
- A Match/Categorize toggle defaults to Match when a match is found.

Bulk work:
- Group by any column; post, edit, exclude or request more information in bulk.

Statement import:
- Upload a PDF or image of a bank statement; extracted transactions are shown beside the original for checking.
- Not supported: non-English statements, statements covering more than one account.

Reconciliation (beta at the time):
- Three-way match of statement PDF, bank feed and QBO entries.
- Unmatched items can be filtered; paired items show green.
- Flags extra posted transactions, pending transactions and excluded transactions to review.

## Availability
- All tiers from QuickBooks Ledger through Intuit Enterprise Suite.
- Turned on with a "try new banking page" option; live in the US and rolling out internationally.
- Announced at the previous year's Intuit Connect (October).

## What it means for a bookkeeper
- Faster matching of payments to invoices, including one payment covering several invoices.
- More consistent coding because suggestions lean on 24 months of the client's own history.
- Grouping and bulk actions help work through similar items in batches during cleanups.

## Limits / caveats
- Statement upload cannot handle non-English or multi-account statements.
- Reconciliation feature was still in beta.
- The article acknowledges the change is significant and that Intuit is still iterating from feedback.
