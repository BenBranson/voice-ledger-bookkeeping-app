# QuickBooks AI agents announcement (Intuit, October 27, 2025)

Published by Intuit QuickBooks on October 27, 2025. A product announcement, not a how-to; features may have changed since.

## What it is
A set of AI "agents" inside QuickBooks on the Intuit platform, each for one area of work, meant to operate with the owner and their accountant.

## What each agent does (as announced), and plan
- Accounting Agent (enhanced), Essentials and up: texts the user when a card transaction needs a receipt; AI reconciliation that compares an uploaded PDF statement with the books, points out discrepancies and suggests fixes.
- Payroll Agent (new), Essentials and up plus a QuickBooks Payroll subscription: gathers hours, spots anomalies, and prepares a payroll draft for approval, sent by text.
- Sales Tax Agent (coming soon at the time), Plus and up: works out the right rate for products/services, flags issues and recommends fixes before filing.
- Customer Agent (enhanced), Plus and up: finds leads in email, drafts follow-ups, builds estimates from written or voice notes for review.
- Finance Agent (enhanced), Advanced and up (more in Intuit Enterprise Suite): month-end close help, KPI insights, monthly performance summaries, anomaly detection.
- Payments Agent (enhanced), Essentials and up: studies customer payment patterns and drafts recurring invoices.
- Project Management Agent (enhanced), Advanced and up: turns signed contracts into projects and tracks profitability including scope changes and cost increases.

## What it means for a bookkeeper
- Reconciliation: statement-to-books comparison is now built in on Essentials and up.
- Receipt chasing: the agent can request missing receipts from the client.
- Month-end: Finance Agent summaries and anomaly flags, but only on Advanced and up.
- The announcement does not specifically describe transaction categorization.

## Limits / caveats
- The article says the business and its accountant stay in control; outputs are drafts or suggestions to approve.
- Accuracy is claimed to be better but not quantified. The one statistic given, 56% of customers saying Intuit AI helps them make better decisions in less time, is about decisions in general, not bookkeeping accuracy.
- No pricing or rollout dates beyond "coming soon".
