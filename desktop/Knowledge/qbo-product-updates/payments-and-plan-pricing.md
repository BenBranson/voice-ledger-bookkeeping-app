# QuickBooks Payments and QBO plan pricing (product pages, as of 2026-09-27)

Combined from two marketing pages: https://quickbooks.intuit.com/payments/ and https://quickbooks.intuit.com/get-customers/. Prices change; this is a snapshot.

## QuickBooks Payments (payments page)
- No monthly fee or minimum; pay per transaction. Per-transaction rates are NOT listed on this page (it links to "See rates").
- Instant deposit costs an extra 1.75%.
- With a QuickBooks Checking account, eligible payments deposit sooner at no fee.
- Chargeback protection up to $25,000 a year, $10,000 per dispute.
- Businesses processing over $2,500 a month can call to ask about up to 25% off standard rates.
- Plan prices shown: Free $0; Solopreneur $20/month (promo $10 for 3 months); Simple Start $38/month (promo $19 for 3 months).

## QBO plans (get-customers page)
Regular monthly prices (a 50% off for 3 months promo was shown):
- Simple Start $38: 1 user + 2 accountant users.
- Essentials $85: 3 users + 2 accountant users.
- Plus $140: 5 users + 2 accountant users.
- Advanced $340: 25 users + 3 accountant users.
No annual contract; cancel any time.

Feature availability:
- Essentials and up: customer referrals, feedback, work requests, testimonials, appointment scheduling.
- Plus and Advanced: contracts and e-signatures; Customer AI (finds leads in Gmail/Outlook and helps with follow-up).
- Advanced only: Project Management AI, Finance AI, custom KPIs and dashboards.
