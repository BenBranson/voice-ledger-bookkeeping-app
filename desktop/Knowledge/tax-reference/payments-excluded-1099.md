# Understand which payments are excluded from a 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC

Article last updated 8/5/2026. No tax year or dollar threshold is given.

## Purpose / when to use
Explains why some contractor payments must NOT appear on a 1099-NEC or 1099-MISC, and how QuickBooks leaves them out.

## What is excluded and why
Payments by credit card, debit card, or through third-party payment networks such as PayPal. The card company or payment processor reports those on Form 1099-K, so the payer does not.

## How QuickBooks handles it
QBO and QuickBooks Contractor Payments:
- Payments paid from a credit card account in the chart of accounts are excluded.
- A payment is also excluded when its check/Ref number field contains one of these words: Debit, Debitcard, DBT, DBT card, DCard, Debit cd, Visa, Masterc, MC, MCard, Chase, Discover, Diners, Paypal. You can type such a word (e.g. "Debit" or "PayPal") in the Ref/Check no. field yourself to exclude a payment.
- Choosing "Credit Card" as the payment method on an expense does NOT exclude it. The payment must come from an actual credit card account.

QuickBooks Desktop: 1099 Summary report is under Reports > Vendors & Payables; map accounts to 1099 boxes in Preferences > Tax:1099 > Company Preferences.

## Pitfalls
- The payment-method label is not enough; the paying account or the Ref no. text drives the exclusion.
- A small business cannot issue a 1099-K itself; only card companies and payment processors do.

## How to verify
In the Prepare 1099s workflow, view the report of non-reportable payments to check what was excluded.
