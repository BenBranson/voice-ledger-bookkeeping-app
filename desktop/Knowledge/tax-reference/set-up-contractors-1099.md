# Set up contractors for 1099s

Article last updated 8/4/2026. It names no tax year.

## Purpose / when to use
Create contractor records so QuickBooks tracks what they are paid and can prepare their 1099s at year end.

## Steps in QBO
1. All apps > Expenses & Bills > Contractors.
2. Add a contractor.
3. Enter name and email.
4. Tick Email this contractor if the contractor should enter their own details (W-9 information); leave it unticked to enter them yourself. You can finish later from the "waiting for info" link.
5. Add contractor.
QuickBooks then tracks payments to them in the background for 1099 preparation.

## Prerequisites and plan limits
- Ask each contractor for a completed IRS Form W-9.
- The 1099 e-file service only supports contractors with US addresses.

## Thresholds (as stated, no year given)
- A 1099 is needed for contractors paid $600 or more in a year (payments through 2025). For payments made in 2026 onward the threshold is $2,000 (IRS, One Big Beautiful Bill Act).
- Payments made electronically through QuickBooks Payments / by credit card do not count toward that amount.

## Pitfalls
Some payment types and contractor types are excluded; the article points to IRS guidance (see payments-excluded-1099.md).

## How to verify
Review the tracked totals for each contractor before filing.

## Bookkeeper note (not from source)
The $600 figure is the threshold for payments made through tax year 2025. Under the 2025 federal tax law (One Big Beautiful Bill Act), the 1099-NEC/1099-MISC threshold rises to $2,000 for payments made after December 31, 2025 (tax year 2026 forms, filed in early 2027), with inflation indexing after that. Although this article was updated in August 2026, it still says $600. Confirm the current threshold on irs.gov before advising a client.
