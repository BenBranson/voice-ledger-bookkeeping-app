# Understand how Intuit makes Form 1099-K

Article last updated 5/25/2026.

## Purpose / when to use
Explains when Intuit issues a Form 1099-K for QuickBooks Payments activity and where to get it. The form summarises the account's card/payment sales and goes to the account holder, the IRS and some states.

## Thresholds (with years)
- Tax year 2025 onward: a US-based Payments account gets a 1099-K when gross payment card transactions exceed $20,000 AND exceed 200 transactions.
- Before tax year 2025: the threshold Intuit used was $5,000 gross volume.
- Some states set lower thresholds of their own; the article does not list them.

## Steps in QBO / Payments
- Available from the January 31 deadline.
- Merchant Service Center > Activity & Reports > Form 1099-K > Download.
- Taxpayer information can be corrected in the Merchant Service Center up to March 31 each year.

## Pitfalls
- If someone receives an Intuit 1099-K without ever opening a Payments account or taking payments, it may be identity theft; the article says to contact 1099_risk@intuit.com.
- The article does not explain reconciling the 1099-K gross figure to deposits.

## Bookkeeper note (not from source)
A 1099-K reports gross amounts (before refunds, chargebacks and processing fees), so it will usually be higher than net deposits in the bank.
