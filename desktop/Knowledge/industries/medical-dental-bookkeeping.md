# Medical, dental and therapy practices

## Revenue
Book deposits by payer type (insurance, patient, cash) from the practice management software's deposit report. The practice software tracks each patient's balance; the books hold totals.

## Privacy
Keep patient health details out of the books and memos. Bookkeepers who see protected health information may need a business associate agreement under HIPAA; ask the practice.

## Overpayments
Patient or insurance overpayments (credit balances) are refunds owed, not income.

## Costs
Clinical supplies, lab fees, malpractice insurance, and equipment (often financed or leased).

## Red flags
Credit balances left in income, equipment leases not recorded, and deposits that don't tie to the practice software.
