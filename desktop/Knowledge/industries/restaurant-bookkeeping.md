# Restaurants and bars

## Daily sales
Book one daily sales journal from the point-of-sale summary: food sales, beverage and alcohol sales, sales tax, tips, discounts and comps, and how it was paid (card, cash, delivery apps). Then match each bank deposit to it; card deposits arrive a day or two later, net of processing fees.

## Tips
Card tips collected for staff are a liability (Tips Payable) until paid out through payroll; they are not income.

## Food and beverage cost
Track food cost and beverage cost apart. Food cost commonly runs about 28–35% of food sales; a sudden jump points to waste, theft, price increases or unrecorded sales.

## Delivery apps
DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub pay out net of commissions. Record the gross sale and the commission as an expense.

## Red flags
Deposits that don't match daily sales, cash sales missing, tips in income, and inventory never counted.
