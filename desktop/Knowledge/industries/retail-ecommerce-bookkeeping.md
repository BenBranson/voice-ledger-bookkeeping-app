# Retail and e-commerce

## Platform payouts
Shopify, Amazon, Etsy, Stripe and PayPal deposit net amounts. Record gross sales, then fees, refunds, and any sales tax the platform collected, through a clearing account that should end each month at zero. Booking the net payout as sales understates both sales and fees.

## Inventory and cost of goods
- Products bought for resale go to an Inventory Asset account; cost of goods sold is recorded as they sell (QuickBooks inventory, or a periodic adjustment to a count).
- Count inventory at least yearly (more often for big sellers) and adjust the books to the count.
- Compare cost of goods to sales monthly; the percentage should be steady.

## Sales tax across states
Marketplaces such as Amazon and Etsy usually collect and remit sales tax for the seller. Sales through the seller's own website may not be covered; once sales into a state pass its threshold, the seller may need to register there.

## Red flags
Payouts booked as sales, inventory never adjusted, negative inventory, and no sales tax collected in states past their threshold.
