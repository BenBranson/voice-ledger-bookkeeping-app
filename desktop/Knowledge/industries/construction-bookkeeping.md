# Construction and trades

## Job costing
Every job is a QuickBooks project (or customer:job). Materials, subcontractors, labor and equipment rental are coded to the job so the owner sees profit per job, not just per month.

## Billing
- Deposits taken before work starts are a liability (Customer Deposits) until the work is done.
- Progress billing on long jobs can run ahead of the work (overbilling) or behind it (underbilling); larger contractors track this on a work-in-progress schedule.
- Retainage held back by the customer is a receivable until released.

## Subcontractors
Collect a W-9 and a certificate of insurance before paying any sub. Subs paid $2,000 or more in a year (2026 onward) need a 1099-NEC.

## Red flags
Deposits booked as income on receipt, subs paid without paperwork, materials for one job charged to another, and equipment purchases expensed.
