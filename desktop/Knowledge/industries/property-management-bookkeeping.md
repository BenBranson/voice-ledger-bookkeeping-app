# Rental real estate and property management

## By property
Use a class or location per property so each has its own profit and loss.

## Tenant money
Security deposits are owed back to tenants: a liability, never income. Property managers holding owners' or tenants' money keep it in trust or escrow accounts as state law requires.

## Mortgages
Split each payment into principal (reduces the mortgage liability), interest (expense) and escrow for taxes and insurance (an asset until the lender pays them).

## Buildings
Record the purchase price split between building (depreciated) and land (not depreciated). Improvements are capitalized; repairs are expensed.

## Red flags
Security deposits in income, mortgage payments fully expensed, and no per-property tracking.
