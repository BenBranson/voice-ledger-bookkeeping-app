# Professional services (consulting, agencies, law, design)

## Revenue
Invoice by client or engagement. Retainers received before the work is done are a liability (Client Retainers or Unearned Revenue) until earned. Law firms hold client trust money in separate trust accounts that follow state bar rules; those funds are never the firm's income.

## Costs
Contract labor (freelancers, 1099s), software subscriptions, professional liability insurance, and reimbursable expenses billed back to clients (tracked apart from fee income).

## Red flags
Retainers booked as income on receipt, the owner's personal subscriptions in the business, and contractor payments without W-9s.
