# Home and field services (cleaning, landscaping, pest control, HVAC)

## Revenue
Separate recurring service plans from one-off jobs. Prepaid plans are a liability (Customer Deposits) until the service is delivered.

## Costs
Job materials and chemicals, field labor, fuel, vehicle repairs and equipment. Trucks, mowers and major tools are fixed assets.

## Seasonality
Many of these businesses swing with the seasons; the 13-week cash forecast helps plan payroll and loan payments through slow months.

## Red flags
Prepaid plans booked as income up front, personal vehicle use in business expenses, and crews paid as contractors when they work like employees.
