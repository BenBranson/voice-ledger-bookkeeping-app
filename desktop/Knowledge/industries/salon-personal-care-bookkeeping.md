# Salons, spas and personal care

## Daily sales
Book daily from the booking system: services, retail products (usually taxable), tips, sales tax, and payment types.

## Workers
Know who is an employee (on payroll, commissions through payroll) and who rents a booth or chair (independent, pays rent to the salon, gets a 1099 if paid by the salon). Booth rent received is income.

## Tips
Card tips are owed to staff: a liability until paid out.

## Red flags
Booth renters paid like employees or the reverse, tips in income, and retail product sales without sales tax.
