# Banking and Accounting Level 1: Lesson 4 - Occasional Tasks

## Overview
Lesson 4 covers occasional (monthly) banking and accounting tasks in QuickBooks Online: bank reconciliation and books review at month-end.

## KEY TOPIC 1: Reconciling a Bank Account

### Introduction to Reconciliation

**What is bank reconciliation?**
- Process of comparing bank and credit card accounts in QuickBooks with actual bank/credit card statements
- Ensures records match what the bank shows was deposited
- Helps catch transactions that deposited but never got cleared
- Useful for finding duplicate transactions accidentally entered

**Purpose:**
- Verify accuracy of bank records
- Match QuickBooks accounts with actual bank statements
- Identify discrepancies and missing transactions

### Reconciliation Frequency & Timing

- **How often:** Typically monthly (at month-end)
- **When to do:** After receiving bank statement and reviewing transactions outside QuickBooks
- **Best practice:** Create a routine schedule for consistency

### Starting the Reconciliation

**Accessing Bank or Credit Card Statements**
- If account is connected to QuickBooks, easily access statement information
- Need statement's ending balance
- Need statement ending date
- Some banks allow automatic feed; others require manual statement upload

**Manual Upload of Statements**
- Upload if bank account not automatically connected
- Upload at end of reconciliation when prompted, OR select "History by account" option
- Best practice: Creates robust paper trail for review and audit purposes

### The Reconciliation Process

**Main Reconciliation Dialog includes:**
- "Which account do you want to reconcile?" dropdown selector
- Account selection
- Beginning balance field
- Statement ending balance field
- Statement ending date field
- "Start reconciliation" button to begin matching

### Matching and Clearing Items

**Exact Matching:**
- Happens when QuickBooks matches (or suggests matches) between:
  - A transaction on Bank transactions screen AND
  - A transaction already recorded in QuickBooks account
- When match is accepted:
  - No new transaction created
  - Note added to original transaction indicating bank feed match
  - Bank transaction moved from "Pending" to "Posted" status

**Reconciliation Status:**
- Pending: Transactions waiting to be matched
- Posted: Transactions matched and cleared

### Troubleshooting Reconciliation Discrepancies

**Seven-Step Troubleshooting Process** (when reconciliation doesn't balance):

1. **Step 1:** Review the beginning balance
2. **Step 2:** Check the ending balance you entered
3. **Step 3:** Combine payments into a single deposit amount
4. Steps 4-7: (refer to course for additional troubleshooting steps)

**Handling Forced Reconciliations:**
- QuickBooks can force a reconciliation when it doesn't balance to zero
- Creates a "Reconciliation Adjustment" entry to represent the difference
- This appears on Profit and Loss under "Reconciliation Discrepancy" account
- Not ideal; indicates unresolved discrepancy

**Undoing a Reconciliation (Best Practice for Complex Issues)**
- If account has many adjustments, consider undoing and starting over
- Allows cleaner identification of discrepancies
- Only accountant users can undo reconciliation
- Process: Go to History by account in Reconcile screen
- Best practice: Download reconciliation as PDF before undoing (Action column has PDF option)
- May reveal discrepancies each month depending on client's business

**Using Audit Log for Investigation**
- Access from General settings gear (top right of screen)
- Lists all changes to reconciliation
- Helpful for understanding why discrepancies occurred
- Good starting point for deeper investigation

### Viewing the Reconciliation Report

**Report Availability:**
- Run reconciliation report for every reconciliation done
- Access via History by account button on Reconcile screen
- Option to Save as PDF (best practice)

**Report Sections:**

**Title and Change Report:**
- Shows client account, bank account, and period ending date
- Displays date range of reconciliation
- Shows "Reconciliation Discrepancy" entry if difference exists
- Lists any changes made during reconciliation
- If changes exist, may need to refer to Audit Log
- General rule: Investigate why changes were made

### Reconciliation Best Practices

- Download reconciliation reports as PDF for audit trail
- Use History by account feature to track reconciliation activities
- Understand why discrepancies occur
- For complex issues, consider undoing and reconciling cleanly
- Use Audit Log to investigate changes
- Document any adjustments or discrepancies found
- Create routine monthly reconciliation schedule

## KEY TOPIC 2: Reviewing the Books at Month-End

### Introduction to Books Review

**What is Books Review?**
- A QuickBooks screen that guides you through main tasks to ensure books are current and accurate
- Available only to accountants via Intuit Accountant Suite (or QuickBooks Online Accountant)
- Clients cannot see this screen
- Monthly review recommended

**Purpose:**
- Systematic check of account status
- Verification that books are up-to-date
- Identification of items needing attention before closing month

### Books Review Screen Overview

**Books Review Feature:**
- Monthly view available
- Transaction review section showing various categories
- Each category shows number of items needing review
- Organized by type of transaction/issue

### Transaction Review Categories

**Standard Categories Checked:**
1. **Bank Transactions** - items within 90 days (note: timeframe adjustable by client)
2. **Uncategorized Transactions** - transactions without proper categorization
3. **Transactions without Entities** - transactions not assigned to customer/vendor
4. **Unposted Transactions** - transactions in pending/draft status
5. **Unpublished Payroll** - payroll items not yet finalized

### Additional Items to Review Monthly

**Areas to Check Each Month:**
- Personal transactions (screen for owner/personal items)
- Any client-specific items flagged for review
- Changes from previous month
- Items that require regular monitoring

### Books Review Workflow

**Process:**
1. Open Books review screen
2. Review highlighted items by category
3. Address each flagged transaction
4. Categorize, assign entities, or post as needed
5. Verify completion for month-end close
6. Document any issues found

### Common Findings in Books Review

- Uncategorized or miscategorized transactions
- Transactions assigned to wrong customer/vendor
- Unposted or pending transactions
- Personal mixed with business transactions
- Missing supporting documentation

### Reconciliation and Books Review Relationship

- **Reconciliation** focuses on matching bank records
- **Books Review** covers overall account accuracy and completeness
- Both are part of end-of-month close procedures
- Typically done in sequence: reconciliation first, then books review

## Month-End Accounting Workflow

1. Receive and review bank/credit card statements
2. Reconcile each account (match QuickBooks to bank)
3. Address any reconciliation discrepancies
4. Run Books review to check account completeness
5. Address flagged items in Books review
6. Close month-end in QuickBooks
7. Generate financial reports for client

## Summary

Lesson 4 emphasizes the importance of two critical monthly tasks:
- **Bank Reconciliation:** Ensures cash accounts match bank statements and catches errors/discrepancies
- **Books Review:** Systematic check that all accounts are accurate, current, and properly categorized before month-end close

Both are essential for accurate financial records and successful month-end closing in QuickBooks.
