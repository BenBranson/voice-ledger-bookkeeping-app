Notes taken directly from the lesson page. The course player was very unstable
during this review (it repeatedly and unpredictably crashed back to the
certification list, or jumped into a different in-progress course, regardless of
which element was clicked) so this lesson could not be scrolled through
end-to-end in one pass the way the task asked. What follows is everything that
was reliably captured across many re-entries, plus an honest note of what could
not be confirmed.

"Get oriented around the **Sales & Get Paid** app and the **Customer Hub**, and
explore the tasks to get up and running with sales and customers in Intuit
QuickBooks."

## Section 1: Tour of the Sales & Get Paid app
Intro paragraph (confirmed):
"The **Sales & Get Paid** app is the place for managing sales-related
activities. Clients can easily access and track invoices, sales receipts,
payments, and their products and services all in one place, streamlining the
sales process.

Not all clients will use this app because it won't always be right for their
business. For example, a medical practice needs to comply with strict
regulations around patient data, so it wouldn't be appropriate to have those
patients showing up in QuickBooks as customers. But, as an accounting pro, it's
valuable for you to understand how to best use the Sales & Get Paid app so that
you can recommend it to those clients who would benefit."

This opens into an 11-step interactive walkthrough (screenshot of the Craig's
Design and Landscaping sample company, with a "How to navigate" callout: "Use
the forward arrow in the text box to step through this..."). Only step 2 of 11
was captured before the player became unstable:

- **Step 2 of 11**: "There are several screens that sit in the Sales & Get Paid
  app, each with a different focus. First up is the **Overview** dashboard. It
  displays a high-level view of what's going on in this app -- including the
  **Sales & get paid funnel** and **Income over time**. You can drill down into
  each area to view details. You can customize the widgets displayed on this
  screen along with the **Create actions** options, so you and your clients can
  get to commonly used items directly."
- Sidebar items visible in the screenshot behind the callout: Accounting,
  Expenses & Bills, **Sales & Get Paid** (highlighted/current), with sub-items
  Overview, Sales transactions, Invoices, Payment links, Recurring payments,
  Sales orders, Sales channels, QuickBooks payments, Products & services;
  Customer Hub, Payroll, Time, Reports, Accounting tools, Privacy.
- Steps 3-11 of this walkthrough were not able to be captured -- the player
  reliably crashed back to the certification list within a few seconds of

  Given the visible sidebar, the remaining steps almost certainly tour: Sales

  channels, QuickBooks Payments, and Products & services -- i.e., the same set
  of sub-screens named in the left nav -- but this is inference from the
