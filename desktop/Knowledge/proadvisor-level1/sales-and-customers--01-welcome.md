## Opening summary
Versatility is what Intuit QuickBooks is all about. It scales as businesses grow,
and clients can choose the features that best suit their needs. Some clients won't
use QuickBooks for sales workflows, but for those who do, it's the starting point
of their data's journey. A client's QuickBooks account populates as sales
transactions -- like invoices and sales receipts -- are recorded in the system and
flow through to be matched, categorized, reconciled, and reported on.

## Learning goals (what the Sales and customers exam covers)
By completing this course, you should be able to:
- Describe how to manage the products and services list and the customers list
- Describe how to customize a client's account by adjusting settings
- Use estimates, invoices, and sales receipts appropriately
- Apply the appropriate method to record customer payments and noncustomer
  deposits
- Explain when to use customer statements, credit memos, and customer refunds
- Implement recurring transactions
- Identify relevant reports for the sales and customers workflow
