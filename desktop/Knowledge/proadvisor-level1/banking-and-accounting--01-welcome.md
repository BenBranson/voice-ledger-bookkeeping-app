# Banking and accounting: Welcome (course overview)

My own summary of the opening lesson. It is taken from the video transcript and the page text, not from watching the video.

## What the course is about
Banking is where the client's bank data meets the data already in QuickBooks. Intuit AI suggests how each bank transaction should be matched or categorized. Keeping the bank feed current makes daily work consistent and the month-end reconciliation easy.

## Structure (it follows the accountant's working rhythm)
1. **Setup tasks**: connect bank and credit card accounts, and set up bank rules.
2. **Frequent tasks**: manage the bank feed day to day.
3. **Occasional tasks**: month-end work, reviewing transactions with issues, reconciling accounts.
4. **Reports** for banking and accounting, then a worksheet to reflect on and plan next steps.

## Learning goals (what the exam covers)
- Connecting bank and credit card accounts to QuickBooks
- Setting up bank rules
- Managing the bank transactions list
- Reconciling accounts
- Using the **Books review** feature at month end

## Add-on products the course mentions (extra fees)
- QuickBooks Bill Pay, QuickBooks Checking, QuickBooks Payments. Eligibility, credit, and application approval apply, and a QBO or QBOA subscription is required. Money movement is by Intuit Payments Inc.
