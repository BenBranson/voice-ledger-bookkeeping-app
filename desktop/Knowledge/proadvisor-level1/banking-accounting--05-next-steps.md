# Banking and Accounting Level 1: Lesson 5 - Next Steps

## Overview
Lesson 5 is a concluding module focusing on reflection about what you've learned and how to apply the Banking and Accounting concepts to your day-to-day role as an accountant or bookkeeper.

## Section 1: Next Steps - Introduction

**Purpose:**
Now's your opportunity to reflect on what you've covered and consider how you can use it to strengthen your day-to-day workflows.

## Section 2: Over to You - Practical Application

You've almost reached the end of this course. Now is a good time to go to the **Level 1 Banking and accounting worksheet** and use it to help you think about how to apply what you've learned to your role.

### Consider Your Context:
- Your day-to-day role
- Your workflows  
- The clients you serve

### Reflection Questions (from worksheet):

Use the worksheet to note down your responses to the following questions:

1. **Trust but Verify:** What will you do to help ensure you trust but verify all suggestions QuickBooks makes to you during the banking and accounting workflows?

2. **Robust Zero:** What checks and balances will you use to ensure that when you get a reconciliation to zero, it's a robust zero?

3. **Multi-Account Reconciliation:** Suppose your firm currently reconciles all accounts down to the penny. What will you do if you want to scale back reconciliation to zero?

4. **Review Planning:** What can you do to plan out the reviews for your clients? Are there any tasks you can add to Books review to make them more consistent?

5. **Data Provision:** What information could you provide through management reports that would add value to your clients?

6. **Training:** If your clients use QuickBooks themselves, have books reviews revealed any common issues or training areas? How will you address these?

## Key Takeaways from Banking and Accounting Level 1

### Reconciliation Best Practices:
- Ensure reconciliation is "robust" (not just at zero due to forced entries)
- Verify all QuickBooks suggestions carefully
- Create a routine monthly reconciliation schedule
- Document any adjustments or discrepancies
- Use audit logs for investigation

### Books Review Application:
- Systematic check of account status monthly
- Verification that books are up-to-date and accurate
- Identification of items needing attention before month-end close

### Client Management:
- Provide value through management reports
- Identify common training needs through reviews
- Plan scalable review processes for multiple clients
- Share security best practices with clients

### Workflow Optimization:
- Integrate bank rules for categorization
- Use management reports for client insights
- Plan reviews based on client needs and business complexity
- Scale reconciliation processes appropriately
