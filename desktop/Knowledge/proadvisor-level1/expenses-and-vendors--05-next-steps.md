# Expenses and vendors: Next steps (course wrap-up)

My own summary of the closing lesson. It has no new procedures; it recaps the course and collects resources.

## Takeaways
- Know the client's vendors, and keep vendor records complete (contact details, remit-to address, terms, TIN, 1099 flag, default category).
- Record the payee on every expense.
- Use automation (autofill, bank feeds, recurring transactions, Bill Pay), but review what it produces.
- Choose the transaction type by timing: expense or check when paid now, bill then bill payment when paid later, a PO first if the client wants one.

## Questions the course asks you to think about (worksheet)
- What do you need to do next to get up to speed on these workflows?
- How will you teach clients best practice for adding vendors who are contractors, and for recording the correct payee on each expense?
