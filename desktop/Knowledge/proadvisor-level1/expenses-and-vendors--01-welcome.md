# Expenses and vendors: Welcome (course overview)

Summary in my own words of the opening lesson of the Level 1 "Expenses and vendors" course.

## What the course is about
Businesses buy goods and services all the time, from one-off purchases like a vehicle to recurring bills like internet service. The course covers how to record that spend in QuickBooks Online (QBO) so the client knows what they spend, where it goes, and keeps cash flowing for future purchases.

## Lesson structure
1. Welcome
2. Setup tasks: vendor list, settings that affect expenses
3. Frequent tasks: day-to-day expense, bill and payment entry
4. Occasional tasks: refunds, vendor credits, credit card payments, recurring transactions
5. Next steps: reports and the exam (the exam is taken separately)

## Learning goals (what the exam tests)
- Managing the vendors list
- Customizing a client's company by adjusting settings (Expenses settings)
- Using purchase orders
- Recording an expense with the right transaction type
- Entering and paying bills
- Uploading bills and receipts
- Recording vendor refunds and vendor credits
- Paying down credit cards
- Recurring transactions
- Reports for the expenses and vendors workflow

## Add-on products the course mentions (extra fees)
- QuickBooks Bill Pay (subscription, eligibility and approval required; money movement by Intuit Payments Inc.)
- QuickBooks Contractor Payments (next-day direct deposit if submitted before the 5 PM PT cutoff)
- QuickBooks Business Network (connect with other QuickBooks businesses in your region)
- Unlimited 1099 e-file (1099-MISC and 1099-NEC for payments recorded in QuickBooks; current filing year only; no state filings; Bill Pay Basic users pay standard 1099 e-file pricing)
