# Internal controls for small businesses

## Separation of duties
The person who records transactions should not be the only one who approves payments and reconciles the bank. In a tiny business, the owner reviewing the bank statement and reconciliation each month covers much of this.

## Access
- Give each person their own QuickBooks login with only the access they need.
- The accountant user (the bookkeeper's firm) should be separate from the owner's login.
- Set a closing date with a password after each month is reviewed.

## Evidence
- Attach receipts and bills to transactions.
- Keep reconciliation reports.
- Review the audit log in QuickBooks when balances change in closed months.

## Warning signs
Unexplained transfers, checks to unfamiliar payees, vendors with only a PO box, round-number payments, duplicate vendor records, and edits to reconciled transactions.
