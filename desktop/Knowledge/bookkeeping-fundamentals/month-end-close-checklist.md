# Month-end close checklist

## Order of work
1. Sync and confirm the data is current.
2. Clear the bank feed: categorize or match every downloaded transaction for the month.
3. Enter anything the bank shows that the books don't (missing bank lines).
4. Fix uncategorized and miscategorized transactions; Uncategorized Expense/Income and Ask My Accountant should be empty or explained.
5. Reconcile every bank and credit card account to the statement, difference 0.00.
6. Clear Undeposited Funds: every payment grouped into its real deposit.
7. Review Accounts Receivable aging: apply payments and credits to the right invoices, follow up on anything over 60 days, consider bad-debt write-offs with the client.
8. Review Accounts Payable aging: every paid bill shows paid; apply vendor credits; nothing paid twice.
9. Check payroll: liabilities recorded and paid; payroll clearing accounts at zero.
10. Sales tax: collected tax matches the liability; the return for the period is filed and paid.
11. Post adjusting entries: depreciation, prepaid expenses used up, accrued expenses, loan interest split.
12. Look for duplicates, negative balances, suspense or clearing accounts with balances, and Opening Balance Equity.
13. Review the Profit and Loss against last month and last year; investigate big swings.
14. Review the Balance Sheet: every balance makes sense and ties to a statement or schedule.
15. Close the period (set a closing date with a password) so nothing changes after review.
16. Produce the client package: financial statements, open questions, and what changed.

## Catch-up months vs a normal month
For a new client behind on their books, start with the oldest unreconciled month and work forward one month at a time, reconciling as you go. Fix opening balances first, because every later month depends on them.
