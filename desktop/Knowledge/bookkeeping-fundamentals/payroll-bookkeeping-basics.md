# Payroll bookkeeping basics

## The pieces of a payroll
- Gross wages: Wages or Salaries expense (officer wages separately for corporations).
- Employee withholdings (federal income tax, Social Security, Medicare, benefit deductions): not expenses; they are liabilities until paid to the government or the benefit provider.
- Net pay: what employees receive from the bank.
- Employer taxes (employer Social Security and Medicare, federal and state unemployment): Payroll Tax expense plus a liability until paid.

## Common recording problems
- A payroll service withdrawal recorded as one lump expense: wages and taxes are mixed, and liability accounts never get used. Split it from the payroll register.
- Tax payments recorded as expense when the liability was already recorded: expenses are doubled and the liability goes negative.
- Payroll liability accounts that grow every month: tax payments are being recorded somewhere else.

## Contractors are not payroll
Independent contractors are paid without withholding and may need a 1099-NEC (2,000 dollars or more paid in a year for payments made in 2026 onward; 600 dollars through 2025). Whether a worker is an employee or a contractor depends on the working relationship, not the job title.

## Records
Keep employment tax records at least 4 years after the tax is due or paid, whichever is later (IRS Publication 583).
