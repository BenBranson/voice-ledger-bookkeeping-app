# Catch-up and cleanup engagements

## Gather first
- Every bank, credit card and loan statement for the period being cleaned up, plus the month before it.
- The last filed tax return and any CPA adjusting entries.
- Payroll reports, sales tax returns, 1099s issued.
- A list of vendors, customers, owners and how the owner takes money out.

## Order of work
1. Lock in the starting point: confirm opening balances against the prior year's tax return and statements. Clear Opening Balance Equity.
2. Work month by month from the oldest month forward.
3. In each month: bank feed, missing transactions, categorization, then reconcile every account to 0.00.
4. Fix receivables and payables so the aging reports match reality.
5. Payroll and sales tax liabilities match the filed returns.
6. Post adjusting entries.
7. Close each finished month with a closing date so it stays put.

## Communicating with the client
Batch questions instead of asking one at a time. Give the client the transaction date, amount, and what you think it is, and ask them to confirm. Keep a list of what changed and why.

## Pricing a cleanup
Quote cleanup separately from the ongoing monthly fee. Volume of transactions, number of accounts, months behind, and payroll or sales tax complexity drive the hours.
