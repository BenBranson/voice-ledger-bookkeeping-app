# Clearing, suspense and catch-all accounts

## What they are for
Temporary holding places. At month end each should be at zero, or hold only items that are understood and listed.

## Undeposited Funds
Holds customer payments until they are grouped into a bank deposit. Anything older than a couple of weeks is a red flag: usually the deposit was also recorded directly to income, so income is double counted. Fix by deleting the duplicate deposit and depositing the payment from Undeposited Funds.

## Payroll clearing
Some payroll setups post the gross payroll to a clearing account and the bank withdrawal out of it. A balance left over means part of the payroll (taxes, net pay, or fees) was not recorded or was recorded twice.

## Suspense, Ask My Accountant, Uncategorized Income and Expense
Places for transactions nobody has identified yet. Build a list of the open items and ask the client; never leave them in the final month-end reports.

## Reconciliation Discrepancies
Created by QuickBooks when a reconciliation is finished with a difference. Any amount here is an unexplained error; find the real cause in the reconciliation and reverse the adjustment.

## Opening Balance Equity
Should be zero after setup. Move confirmed balances to the right equity account (Owner's Equity or Retained Earnings) with the CPA's agreement.
