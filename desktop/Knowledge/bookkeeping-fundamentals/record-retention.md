# How long to keep business records

## The general rule
Keep records that support income or deductions until the period of limitations for that tax return runs out.

## Periods of limitations (IRS Publication 583, Table 3)
- 3 years: the normal period.
- 6 years: if income was underreported by more than 25 percent of the gross income shown.
- 7 years: for a claim for a loss from worthless securities or a bad debt deduction.
- No limit: if no return was filed, or a fraudulent return was filed.
- Refund claims: the later of 3 years after filing or 2 years after the tax was paid.

## Other rules
- Employment tax records: at least 4 years after the tax is due or paid, whichever is later.
- Property and asset records: until the limitation period ends for the year the asset is sold or disposed of.

## What counts as support
Bank deposit slips, invoices, receipts, credit card slips, canceled checks or bank statements showing payee, amount and date, and Forms 1099. Electronic records are acceptable if they are complete, legible, indexed and retrievable.
