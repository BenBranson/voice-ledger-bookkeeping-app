# Common errors and how to find them

## Duplicates
The same expense entered twice, or entered by hand and then also accepted from the bank feed. Look for the same vendor, amount and date. Keep the one matched to the bank line, delete or void the other.

## Transposition and slides
- Transposition: digits swapped (1,420 entered as 1,240). The error amount is divisible by 9.
- Slide: decimal point moved (142.00 entered as 1,420.00). The error amount is also divisible by 9.

## Reversed entries
A deposit entered as a payment or the reverse: the difference is twice the amount.

## Wrong account
An expense coded to the wrong category is fixed by recategorizing; it does not change totals, only where they show. A transfer between the business's own accounts coded as income or expense is a common one: moving money from savings to checking is not income.

## Personal spending in the business account
Owner purchases paid from the business account belong in Owner's Draw (sole proprietor or partnership) or a shareholder distribution or loan (corporation), not in business expenses. Ask the client; do not guess.

## Payments without the document they pay
A customer payment with no invoice, or a bill payment with no bill, leaves a negative receivable or payable. Create or find the invoice or bill and link the payment.

## Missing transactions
A bank line with nothing posted in the books. Enter it from the bank statement, with the receipt if available.

## Opening balances
Accounts added with an opening balance post the offset to Opening Balance Equity. It should be zero after setup is finished; reclassify it to the proper equity or balance account once the starting balances are confirmed.
