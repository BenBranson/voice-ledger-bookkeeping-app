# Texas franchise tax

## Who it applies to
Entities formed in Texas or doing business in Texas: LLCs, corporations, partnerships, and most other legal entities. Sole proprietors (individuals) and general partnerships made up only of natural persons are generally not taxable entities.

## No tax due threshold
For 2026 and 2027 reports the no tax due threshold is 2,650,000 dollars of annualized total revenue. An entity at or below it owes no franchise tax and, since reports due January 1, 2024 and later, does NOT file a franchise tax report or a No Tax Due Report. It still must file a Public Information Report (corporations and LLCs) or an Ownership Information Report (other entities) every year.

## Due date
May 15 each year, moved to the next business day if it falls on a weekend or holiday. Extensions are available if requested by May 15.

## Rates (2026-2027)
- 0.75 percent of taxable margin for most entities.
- 0.375 percent for qualifying retail and wholesale businesses.
- EZ computation: 0.331 percent of apportioned revenue, for entities with total revenue of 20 million dollars or less.

## Taxable margin
The CPA chooses the method that gives the lowest margin: 70 percent of total revenue, total revenue minus cost of goods sold, total revenue minus compensation, or total revenue minus 1 million dollars.

## Bookkeeping role
Filing is the CPA's or the client's job (Voice Ledger's agreement excludes tax filings). The bookkeeper makes it easy: revenue clean and complete, cost of goods sold and compensation in their own accounts, and the year closed on time. Remind clients that the Public Information Report is due May 15 even when no tax is owed; a missed report brings a 50 dollar late penalty and can lead to forfeiture of the entity's right to do business.
