# Owner equity, draws and distributions

## Sole proprietors and single-member LLCs
The owner takes money out as Owner's Draw, an equity account with a debit balance. Draws are not expenses and are not wages. Money the owner puts in is Owner's Investment (or Owner Contributions).

## Partnerships and multi-member LLCs
Each partner has their own draw and capital accounts.

## S corporations
Owner-employees should be paid wages through payroll; additional money taken out is a shareholder distribution (equity). Personal expenses paid by the company go to distributions or a shareholder loan, not business expense. Reasonable compensation is the CPA's decision.

## C corporations
Owners are paid wages or dividends; personal spending is a shareholder loan or dividend.

## Owner loans
A loan from the owner to the business is a liability (Loan from Owner); a loan from the business to the owner is an asset. Document them; repayments move the balance, not income or expense.

## Year end
Net income closes into Retained Earnings automatically in QuickBooks. Draw and distribution accounts are often closed into equity by the CPA's year-end entries.
